Make your gitpin key

Your key lets your repo sign the announcements that tell cid.contact where your files are. It is made in this browser and never sent anywhere.

Peer ID:

  1. In your repo on GitHub open Settings > Secrets and variables > Actions.
  2. Click "New repository secret".
  3. Name: GITPIN_KEY. Secret: paste the key.
  4. Click "Add secret".

Never commit this key or paste it anywhere public; anyone with it can publish as your pins.

The peer ID is public; it identifies your pins on cid.contact.
